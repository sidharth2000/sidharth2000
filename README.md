### WallFluxer AI

**One sentence. One click. A wallpaper worth keeping.**

A Windows desktop app that turns a simple idea into stunning **4K wallpapers**, running fully on your own GPU. No subscriptions, no cloud, no queue.

[![Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/View_Repo-WallFluxerAI-00F5FF?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=0D1117)](https://github.com/sidharth2000/WallFluxerAI)
[![Backend](https://img.shields.io/badge/Backend-WallFluxerAI--API-B026FF?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/sidharth2000/WallFluxerAI-API)

</div>

- 🎨 **One-sentence wallpapers:** describe an idea and the app builds the optimised prompt from style, theme and composition presets
- 🔬 **Custom multi-stage pipeline:** Stable Diffusion 1.5 base generation, hires fix for real detail, then Real-ESRGAN upscaling to 4K and beyond
- 🔒 **100% local and offline:** generation runs on your own NVIDIA GPU (3 GB VRAM or more)
- 📦 **Zero-setup distribution:** a local FastAPI engine packaged with PyInstaller that the desktop app starts and stops by itself
- 🧰 **Bring your own models and presets**, plus a built-in gallery to manage everything you generate
